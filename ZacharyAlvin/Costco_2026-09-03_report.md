## Executive Summary

Costco remains a high-quality retailer. From fiscal 2023 through fiscal 2025, net sales increased from $237.710 billion to $269.912 billion, membership-fee revenue increased from $4.580 billion to $5.323 billion, and net income increased from $6.292 billion to $8.099 billion. These figures imply two-year compound annual growth rates of approximately 6.6%, 7.8%, and 13.5%, respectively (my calculations from Costco's reported figures). [Costco 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

The momentum continued into fiscal 2026. For the 36 weeks ended May 10, 2026, net sales rose 9.6% to $203.374 billion, while third-quarter comparable sales increased 6.6% after excluding gasoline-price and foreign-exchange effects. Digitally enabled comparable sales increased 20.8% on the same adjusted basis in the quarter. [Costco Q3 FY2026 earnings release](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-and-Year-To-Date-Operating-Results-For-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx)

However, a strong company is not automatically an attractive stock. As of the September 1 valuation date, Costco had not yet reported complete fiscal 2026 earnings; the fourth-quarter earnings release and call were scheduled for September 24, 2026. The report therefore lacks the audited year-end balance sheet, full-year cash flow, and management's updated outlook needed for a disciplined valuation. ## Why the Business Deserves a Premium

Costco's model combines limited assortment, high purchasing volume, rapid inventory turnover, and low merchandise margins. The company says this structure allows it to operate profitably at lower gross margins than most retailers; it also often sells inventory before paying suppliers. [Costco 2023 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983223000042/cost-20230903.htm)

Membership economics make that model more durable. Membership-fee revenue increased in each of the last three reported fiscal years, and fiscal 2025 renewal rates remained high at 92.3% in the United States and Canada and 89.8% worldwide. Costco noted that the growing mix of online sign-ups, which renew at a slightly lower rate, pressured the reported renewal figures. [Costco 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

The balance sheet adds resilience. At August 31, 2025, Costco held $14.161 billion of cash and equivalents and $1.123 billion of short-term investments, versus approximately $5.788 billion of debt at net carrying value, leaving net cash before lease liabilities. [Costco 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

## Fiscal 2026 Update and Management Commentary

The fiscal third quarter strengthened the operating case. Net income increased 15% to $2.192 billion, membership-fee revenue increased 10.7% to $1.373 billion, and paid memberships reached 82.9 million. Renewal rates were 92.2% in the United States and Canada and 89.7% worldwide. [Costco Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000051/cost-20260510.htm)

On the May 28 earnings call, CEO Ron Vachris said Costco was targeting more than 30 net new warehouse openings annually in coming years. He also described faster checkout, expanded same-day delivery, and efforts to improve Costco's visibility in AI-assisted product search. These initiatives may support growth, but they still require execution and capital. [Costco Q3 FY2026 earnings-call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/)

The most recent sales news was also positive. Costco reported fiscal 2026 net sales of $297.3 billion, up 10.2%, and a full-year comparable-sales increase of 8.4%; digitally enabled comparable sales rose 20.9%. This release covered sales only, not the complete income statement or cash-flow statement. [Costco August 2026 sales release](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-August-Sales-Results/default.aspx)

## Risks and Falsification Test

The main business risk is pressure on the value proposition. Costco competes with Walmart, Target, Kroger, Amazon, Sam's Club, BJ's, supermarkets, online retailers, and other formats on price, quality, convenience, location, and service. [Costco 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

Margins are another constraint. In fiscal Q3 2026, reported gross margin fell 21 basis points year over year to 11.04%; excluding gasoline inflation, it increased only one basis point. Management attributed core pressure partly to lower margins in fresh foods and foods and sundries as Costco invested in lower prices, while transportation costs also rose. [Costco Q3 FY2026 earnings-call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/)

Tariffs add uncertainty. Costco said the future impact remained fluid and that it intended to return approved tariff recoveries attributable to amounts passed to members, with timing and amount dependent on refunds and litigation. [Axios, March 5, 2026](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/05/costco-members-tariff-refunds-earnings) [Costco Q3 FY2026 earnings-call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/)

The Lab 03 falsification question was: *What evidence would show that Costco's membership-fee growth and high renewal rates can no longer justify its valuation premium?* The answer is a combination of slowing underlying membership growth, renewal-rate deterioration, weaker traffic, and margin pressure while the market continues to price in superior growth. A practical warning threshold would be two consecutive quarters with worldwide renewal below 89%, adjusted comparable-sales growth below 4%, and membership-fee growth below 5%; these thresholds are my monitoring rules, not company guidance.

## Valuation Discipline

I would not initiate solely from operating quality. A defensible purchase requires the September 1 closing share price, diluted share count, updated net cash, and normalized free cash flow after the fiscal 2026 earnings release. Those inputs should support a bear/base/bull valuation and an expected return that clears the fund's required return after allowing for multiple compression.

This is the key unresolved point: fiscal 2026 sales growth was strong, but sales alone do not establish per-share value. The September 24 earnings release should supply the missing full-year earnings, cash flow, year-end balance sheet, and management commentary. ## Current Call

**Watch-defer (wait for more information).** Costco's durable membership model, rising cash generation, strong fiscal 2026 sales, and net-cash position support continued monitoring, but I would wait for complete fiscal 2026 results and a price-based expected-return analysis before initiating. [Costco 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm) [Costco August 2026 sales release](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-August-Sales-Results/default.aspx)

**What would change the call:** I would initiate if the fiscal 2026 results confirm durable membership and free-cash-flow growth and a bear/base/bull valuation using the September 1 price offers an acceptable expected return; I would move to do not initiate if renewal, traffic, or normalized margins weaken while the valuation remains demanding.
