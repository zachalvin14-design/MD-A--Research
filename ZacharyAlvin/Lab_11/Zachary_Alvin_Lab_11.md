### Output spans

| Output | PPA sales-growth span | PPA operating-margin span | Larger driver over these ranges |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| FY2030 operating profit | $729.2 million | $838.7 million | PPA operating margin |
| FY2030 equipment operations FCFF | $463.9 million | $641.6 million | PPA operating margin |
| Value per share | $20.20 | $32.37 | PPA operating margin |

Over these ranges, PPA operating margin is the larger driver for all three outputs. The margin path changes profit earned on PPA sales without requiring the additional sales-related capex and working capital generated by the growth path. In the actual runs, moving from the lower to higher margin path increased FY2030 operating profit from $7,715.3 million to $8,554.0 million and equipment operations FCFF from $5,761.6 million to $6,403.3 million. “Over these ranges” is important because the larger span can reflect the tested range and does not prove that margin is inherently more important under every possible range.
